# What remains unexplained

## Purpose

This page does not state conclusions. It identifies the points at which the documentary record leaves a material question that a lawyer, witness or expert may be able to answer.

---

## 1. How did “Not clear” become a stable practical division?

The relationship between the neurological syndrome and the feet was expressly unresolved, while Podiatry became the practical route for the structural/local foot problem.

That may have been entirely reasonable.

What requires explanation is what clinical safeguard kept the unresolved neurological relationship alive as a question after the pathways separated.

This matters particularly because the neurologist knew the patient had already received years of chiropody/Podiatry treatment.

---

## 2. What happened when discharge was challenged?

The patient gives a detailed account of challenging discharge because the feet remained unresolved. Four students were present and one allegedly fetched the neurologist.

The event is not independently established.

If corroborated, the important question is not merely why it is absent from the discharge account. The prior question is what the treating neurologist did with the clinical challenge when it was raised.

Did he reconsider the relationship? Did he reconsider discharge? Did he decide that further inpatient work was unnecessary? If so, on what grounds?

---

## 3. Why does the record preserve understanding but not the reported disagreement?

The discharge account repeatedly records the patient's intelligence, insight, ability to question and “good understanding” of the rationale.

The patient says he understood the explanation and nevertheless disputed whether the unresolved feet made discharge sufficient.

If the encounter is established, a later reader would need to distinguish comprehension from agreement.

The version history matters because “good understanding” may have been accurate when first drafted and later become incomplete as a description of the patient's position if a material disagreement occurred before final transmission.

---

## 4. What changed while the discharge document remained open?

The record was capable of post-discharge supplementation: the abnormal DaTscan was added after physical discharge.

That diagnostic update strengthened Parkinsonism but did not resolve the foot relationship.

If the patient's challenge occurred, the document-lifecycle question is precise:

> **What information entered the final account after the decision to discharge, and what material information about the decisional history did not?**

This is a version/audit question, not a motive allegation.

---

## 5. What did the 2017–2018 medication-state evidence do to the original formulation?

Withdrawal and rechallenge supplied patient-specific evidence that the foot/lower-limb domain changed with neurological treatment state.

The record shows treatment adjustment.

What is harder to locate is an explicit reformulation of the original “Not clear” relationship in light of that evidence.

The expert question is whether such reformulation was required and, if so, what it should have changed.

---

## 6. Was the 2021 substitution actually tested against the feet?

Procyclidine had generated foot/lower-limb response evidence. It was removed for legitimate ocular reasons and Sinemet was used as the replacement.

The record does not clearly show a structured test of whether the replacement adequately controlled the symptom domain that had previously been medication-sensitive.

The significance of that documentary gap requires expert interpretation.

---

## 7. What should have happened once OFF-related pain became an explicit neurological interpretation?

By later 2023, Neurology itself described pain and shuffling as OFF-related / undertreated.

That is a stronger proposition than simply observing that medication sometimes helped.

The question is whether this should have caused a broader re-examination of the earlier history, including the 2017 uncertainty, procyclidine withdrawal, 2018 focal foot return, 2021 substitution, 2022 morning pattern, structural findings, pregabalin response and treatment toxicity.

---

## 8. What was the safeguarding response to the 2023 crisis marker?

The GP documented pain “causing death wish”, recorded concern and sought review before “we get into trouble here.” The nurse documented debilitating pain and no quality of life.

The complete record contains no corresponding safeguarding response.

What standard applied? What risk assessment, safety planning, referral or documented safety-netting would reasonably have been required? What, if anything, occurred outside the surviving record?

---

## 9. What was the safeguarding response in 2025?

The treating neurologist personally recorded “I won't live with it” in the same foot-pain narrative in which he recorded medication relief.

He also acted therapeutically by introducing Sinemet CR. The issue is therefore not that the foot problem was wholly ignored.

The question is whether the crisis-level language required an additional safeguarding / risk-management response and whether the severity plus medication responsiveness required a more explicit integrated reassessment.

---

## 10. How should the 2025 retrospective account be used?

The treating neurologist later relied substantially on the longitudinal correspondence and on the 2017 record of the patient's “good understanding.”

Retrospective explanation is not invalid merely because it is retrospective.

But repetition of an earlier proposition is not, by itself, independent confirmation that the underlying question was freshly re-tested at each later point.

For each historical proposition in the 2025 response, the useful questions are:

- What is its native source?
- Was it observed in 2025, quoted from 2017, inherited through later letters or reconstructed retrospectively?
- What independent evidence supports it?
- Does it fairly preserve both treatment-risk evidence and treatment-benefit / under-coverage evidence?

---

## 11. What can the 2026 genetic result legitimately change?

PRKN confirmation establishes the later disease etiology and the genetic basis of the parkinsonism and dystonia.

It strengthens the retrospective relevance of the PARK2 hypothesis and lower-limb dystonia.

It cannot be treated as certainty available in 2017, and it cannot prove that every historical foot symptom had the same cause.

The expert must define its proper retrospective reach.

---

## 12. What period of harm is actually attributable to any breach?

Even if an expert identifies a failure to reassess at some date, causation remains separate.

The broadest theory—that a different 2017 decision would have prevented the later course—requires more counterfactual support than a narrower theory focused on later points when OFF-related pain, timing or crisis severity became explicit.

The legally defensible period of avoidable harm must be established, not assumed.
