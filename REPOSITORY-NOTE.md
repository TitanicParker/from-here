# Repository note

This flat handover repository consolidates the useful work developed across the earlier `once`, `once-more` and `once-again` repositories without requiring the legal reader to learn their internal analytical vocabulary.

The earlier repositories contributed, in plain language:

- from **once**: the conservative longitudinal clinical question, decision points, treatment-benefit versus treatment-risk analysis, narrow therapeutic-window problem, and expert testing discipline;
- from **once-more**: the July 2017 document chronology, post-discharge DaTscan update, distinction between current reinterpretation and historical reconstruction, counterevidence, weaknesses and source-lifecycle questions; and
- from **once-again**: the Day 2 / Day 4 / Day 8 temporal structure, the discharge-decision question, MRI comparator, witness/teaching footprint and the significance of the missing patient challenge.

`from-here` adds the litigation handover, the patient's own statement, the 2023/2025 safeguarding issue, limitation/preservation urgency and a mobile-first swipeable presentation.

The complete record remains controlling. The synthesis is a convenience layer, not a substitute for the sources or independent legal/clinical judgment.
