# Source map

## Purpose

The complete record is the controlling factual substrate. Its top-level document numbers reflect compilation order rather than strict chronology. This page gives a chronological map so a legal reader can move quickly from an event to the underlying source.

---

## 2017

### GP record — April to November 2017

Primary GP export. Early entries include foot/toe symptoms, bilateral foot pain and abnormal gait. Duplicate source entries are preserved rather than silently deduplicated.

### GP urgent referral — 29 June 2017

Primary referral bringing feet, gait, tone, movement initiation, dexterity and neurological concern into one urgent question. Key anchors include `CLIN-000815` onward and the detailed neurological/foot presentation at `CLIN-000847–CLIN-000884`.

### GP / Podiatry contact — 6 July 2017

Records GP contact with Podiatry and priority “as Parkinson's”. Begins around `CLIN-000899`.

### July 2017 discharge correspondence

Extensive patient-facing account of the admission, history, neurological findings, foot findings, diagnostic uncertainty, treatment rationale, plan, MRI and later DaTscan addendum.

Important themes include:

- severe very-young-onset Parkinsonism;
- structural foot abnormalities;
- relationship of foot/leg symptoms described as “Not clear”;
- deliberate dopaminergic restraint;
- possible future regret from delaying levodopa;
- patient described as intelligent, insightful and having “good understanding”; and
- formal Plan/Recommendations incorporating the preceding synthesis.

### Clinician-to-clinician correspondence — Monaghan to Healy

Treating neurologist seeks genetics/specialist advice and asks whether holding back levodopa is appropriate: `CLIN-001719–CLIN-001733`.

### Healy response — 12 September 2017

Specialist opinion considering probable PARK2, recommending testing and discussing low-dose dopamine-replacement benefit versus dyskinesia risk: `CLIN-001734` onward.

### Contemporaneous treatment-response emails — October 2017

Patient/workplace correspondence documenting marked physical improvement on treatment and deterioration during procyclidine withdrawal, including feet/gait changes: approximately `CLIN-001770–CLIN-001834`.

### Medical assessment email — 24 October 2017

Independent workplace-directed contemporaneous account.

### Neurology notes — remainder of 2017

Ongoing medication response, withdrawal and management.

## Using the complete record

The compilation assigns stable `CLIN-` references to material propositions. Where a source is a transcription, retrospective account or later recollection, that status is stated rather than silently converted into contemporaneous fact.

The original source remains controlling.
